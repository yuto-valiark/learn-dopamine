もし冷蔵庫だったら

If I Were a Fridge

お手本音声

“If I were a fridge, I would sleep all day,” Kanato says.

“You would have to stay in the kitchen,” Nozomi tells him.

“If I had wheels, I would go outside,” he says.

The fridge thinks about that.

“If I could go outside, I would visit an ice cream shop.”

“To buy ice cream?” asks Nozomi. “No. To meet the freezer.”
