もし冷蔵庫だったら

If I Were a Fridge

お手本音声

“If I were a fridge, I would sleep all day,” Kanato says. Nozomi tells him he would have to stay in the kitchen.

“If I had wheels, I would go outside,” he says.

Nozomi shows him an ice cream shop on her phone. The seasonal flavors are on sale. Kanato is interested in what flavors they offer. Kanato tries to think about which flavor he would choose. The fridge is looking past the lemon and berry cups at the back of the photo. It wants a better angle. “That looks pretty, but I can't see past the cups in that backlight. Can we look at a photo from a different angle?”

“If I could go outside, I would visit that shop,” says the fridge.

“To buy ice cream?” asks Nozomi.

“No. To meet the freezer.”

“Contact the manager first,” says Kanato. “The freezer might like its privacy.”

“I respect that,” says the fridge. “It can keep the door closed.”

Nozomi turns the phone so the fridge can see the whole photo. “Look. The freezer already has wheels.”

“Then it can come here,” says the fridge. “I was hoping to stay in the kitchen after all.”
