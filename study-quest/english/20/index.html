桃太郎、結末を変える

Momotaro Changes the Ending

お手本音声

Nozomi has already read the old story about Momotaro.

“Our ending should be different,” she says.

In their story, the ogre who lives on the island opens a tiny café.

The sweets are made by Momotaro, and Kanato brings the tea.

One day, the talking fridge walks into the café.

Later, Nozomi asks, “Did the fridge order pudding?”

“No,” says Kanato. “It asked if we had a job opening.”
