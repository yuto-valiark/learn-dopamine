桃太郎、結末を変える

Momotaro Changes the Ending

お手本音声

Nozomi has already read the old story about Momotaro. “Our ending should be different,” she says.

のぞみは桃太郎の昔話をもう読んでいます。「私たちの結末は違うのにしよう」

In their story, the ogre who lives on the island opens a tiny café. The sweets are made by Momotaro, and Kanato brings the tea.

二人の話では、島に住む鬼が小さなカフェを開きます。お菓子は桃太郎が作り、かなとはお茶を持ってきます。

At the opening, a teenage musician plays a flute solo and a local guitarist joins in. A boy on a djembe keeps a steady beat. The melody is so catchy that the ogre makes up the lyrics to a little rap while he works. “Somebody help me with these plates!” he sings.

開店の日、十代の演奏家がフルート独奏。地元のギタリストも加わり、少年がジャンベで一定のリズムを刻みます。覚えやすい曲なので鬼は働きながら短いラップの歌詞を作ります。「誰かお皿を運ぶの手伝って！」

The local musicians attract a small crowd. The teenager puts down her flute and carries two plates to a waiting guest. Someone claps, thinking the delivery is part of the song. The ogre points toward the door, where more guests are arriving. “It looks like a success if you count the guests,” he says. “I also have to count the plates.”

演奏に人が集まり、演奏家はフルートを置いて待つお客へ皿を二枚運びます。演奏の一部だと思った人が拍手します。鬼は次々客が来るドアを指します。「お客の数だけ見ると大成功だ。お皿の数も数えなきゃ」

Just then the talking fridge walks into the café. Every shelf it has is empty.

そこへしゃべる冷蔵庫が来ます。棚はすべて空。

Later, Nozomi asks, “Did the fridge order pudding?”

「冷蔵庫はプリンを注文した？」とあとでのぞみ。

“No,” says Kanato. “It asked if we had a job opening. It can bring everyone's pudding at the same time.”

「ううん。求人があるか聞いたんだ。みんなのプリンを一度に運べるから」とかなと。
