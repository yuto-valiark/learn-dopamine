図工室で見つかった未来の落書き

A Drawing from Next Thursday

お手本音声

Nozomi finds a drawing in the art room. It shows a red mark on the ceiling. Kanato is holding a bucket. At the bottom, the artist has copied the date from a poster for next week's creative arts show. Above the date, a familiar phrase from a well-known mystery appears: “Look Up.”

“Have we made that mark yet?” asks Kanato.

“Look up,” says Yuto. The ceiling is white.

They have already put away the red paint. Nozomi moves the water bucket to the sink so it won't damage the furniture if it spills. By the time the class leaves, the ceiling is still clean.

The following Thursday, the art room is set up for a presentation. The class is hanging its artwork. Yuto uses a technique he learned at a studio: touch a corner to test the paint before moving anything. Yuto stands on a low stool. The picture is on an old piece of cardboard from his stationery drawer. Kanato holds an empty bucket full of clean brushes. Nozomi hands Yuto a painting of a red sun.

“Has the paint dried yet?” she asks. “That drawing inspired us, but we don't have to make the same mark on the ceiling.”

Yuto touches the bottom of the painting with a finger. “This part is dry,” he says. He takes the picture before Nozomi can check the top. “Wait,” she says. “That was a trick in the original drawing, too.”

Yuto turns the painting over. The top edge touches the ceiling. A little part of the sun is still wet. It leaves a red mark shaped like a footprint.

The teacher comes in and asks for a short report. Nozomi has just noticed that Kanato is standing in the same place as the boy in the drawing. He looks at his bucket. Then he writes, “An honest account: one wet picture touched the ceiling. The bucket behaved beautifully.”

Yuto adds one line: “We should have checked the top, too.”
