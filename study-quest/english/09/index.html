小さすぎるかさ

A Very Small Umbrella

お手本音声

When it starts to rain, Nozomi opens an umbrella.

Kanato laughs because the umbrella is very small.

“If we stand close together, it will work,” says Nozomi.

They stand close together. Their bags get wet.

Then a snail passes by.

“Perfect,” says Nozomi. “If you’re a snail, this umbrella is huge.”
