いちばん遅い競走

The Slow Race

お手本音声

Kanato is faster than Nozomi, but today they have a slow race. “The slowest person wins,” says Nozomi.

A turtle rolls up to the finish line on a skateboard and takes out a stopwatch. “The rule is simple. Move forward.”

Kanato takes a small step. Nozomi takes an even smaller step. The turtle looks at their feet and walks past both of them.

“He's faster than both of us!” says Kanato.

Kanato crosses first. Nozomi takes one last step just before the timer rings.

The turtle hands Nozomi her award. “You win. I was afraid it was a two-day race.” She smiles and takes two quick steps back.
