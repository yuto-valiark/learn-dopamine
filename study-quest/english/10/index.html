いちばん遅い競走

The Slow Race

お手本音声

Kanato is faster than Nozomi, but today they have a slow race.

“The slowest person wins,” says Nozomi.

Kanato takes a small step. Nozomi takes an even smaller step.

A turtle walks between them.

“It’s faster than both of us!” says Kanato.

At the finish line, the turtle waits. “Are you coming today or tomorrow?”
