冷蔵庫が学校の最終試験を受ける

The Fridge Takes Its Final Exam

お手本音声

The talking fridge signed up for a final exam at the school. The application form asked for any special qualifications and the name of the candidate's best academic subject. It wrote, “Coolest candidate in the building.” Yuto pointed out that the question probably meant certificates. The fridge added its energy rating.

しゃべる冷蔵庫が期末試験に応募。資格と得意教科を聞かれ「校内で一番クールな受験者」。資格証明のことだとユウトが指摘し、省エネ評価を足します。

The first task was to read an announcement and use a table. A class was visiting a museum. The morning bus could carry twenty passengers, but all twenty-three people in the group had to travel together. The afternoon service was large enough. The fridge remembered a worksheet about saving money and chose the bus that cost less.

最初は案内と表。博物館へ行く二十三人は一緒に乗ります。朝は二十人、午後は十分な定員。節約のプリントを思い出し、安いほうを選びます。

The next task was to listen to a message about lost property. The speaker said that a green bag had been moved to the office. The fridge wrote “green bag” on a receipt. By mistake, it had written over a shopping list for the following day. When Yuto read it back, he heard the words “two tomatoes, one green bag and half a bottle of milk.”

次は忘れ物の放送。「緑のかばんは事務室へ」。レシートの翌日の買い物リストに重ねて「緑のかばん」と書き、ユウトが読み返すと「トマト二個、緑のかばん一個、牛乳半瓶」。

For the final task, each candidate had to explain a decision. “Which detail caused the difficulty?” asked Nozomi. The fridge compared the capacities again. Three passengers wouldn't fit on the morning bus. It had tried to solve it as if it were a basic addition problem from the textbook, but the key was a line of words. “Which condition does your plan break?” Nozomi asked. “If we leave three children at school, I could keep their lunches cold,” it suggested.

最後は決めたことの説明。「どの細部が問題？」と聞かれ、朝は三人が乗れません。教科書の足し算のように考えましたが、鍵は文の一行。「どの条件に反する？」 冷蔵庫は「三人置いていけば弁当は冷やせる」。

“Is keeping their lunches cold more important than taking the whole group?” asked Kanato.

かなとは「一緒に行くより弁当？」。

“Probably,” said the fridge, “but I can see why that answer might not earn full marks.”

「たぶんね。でも、その答えだと満点はもらえないかもしれないって分かるよ」と冷蔵庫は言います。

The fridge flipped over the receipt and tried its answer again. “They should take the afternoon bus. My evidence is one fact right here in the table: the morning bus holds twenty. The group has twenty-three people, and they all have to travel together.”

冷蔵庫はレシートを裏返し、答え直します。「午後のバスに乗るべきです。表の事実は朝の定員二十人。二十三人は一緒に行く必要がある」と答えます。

“You stated your reason clearly this time,” said Nozomi. “That shows you understand why the condition matters.” The fridge had been stressed and unhappy with its first answer. Now it had a plan. It felt more confident. Next to its answer it drew the little star it used to celebrate a personal victory. It hoped that would help it achieve a better grade. “That should motivate me to review the next question,” it said. “My first achievement today.” That evening, the fridge stuck the receipt on its door to remind Yuto to shop. When he got home, he held up a shopping bag. “Two tomatoes and the milk,” he said. “But I couldn't find a green bag in the vegetable section.”

「今は理由がはっきり。なぜ条件が大事か分かったね」とのぞみ。最初は緊張して不満でしたが、今は計画と自信。小さな星を描き、成績がよくなると願います。「次を復習するやる気になる。今日最初の達成」。夕方ユウトが買い物を忘れないようレシートを張ります。「トマト二個と牛乳。野菜売り場で緑のかばんは見つからなかった」と買い物袋を見せます。
