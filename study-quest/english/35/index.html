うちの夕食、宇宙食扱い

Supper for a Satellite

お手本音声

The science museum announced a competition on its website: prepare a meal that someone could eat in a space station. Kanato brought a loaf of bread he had baked from organic flour and a bar of chocolate. An engineer warned that bread with loose crumbs, such as this loaf, would be hard to eat in space. Nozomi brought soup in a bag that she could seal. Yuto brought miso soup in a bowl.

科学館が宇宙ステーションで食べられる食事を作る大会をサイトで発表。かなとは有機小麦粉で焼いたパンと板チョコを持参。技術者はこのパンのようにくずが落ちるものは宇宙で食べにくいと言います。のぞみは密封できる袋のスープ、ユウトはお椀の味噌汁。

“Eating that without spilling it might be difficult,” said Nozomi.

「こぼさず食べるのは難しそう」とのぞみ。

“Gravity will keep it in the bowl,” said Yuto.

「重力があるからお椀に入ったままだよ」とユウト。

“Here, yes.”

「ここではね」

A museum guide showed a video of liquid water floating in little balls. Bread crumbs could float away, too. Kanato cut a thin piece of bread and put it into a bag. The guide showed him how to close a container before letting go of it.

説明員の映像では液体の水が小さな球で浮かびます。パンくずも飛びます。かなとは薄く切って袋に入れ、手を離す前に容器を閉める方法を教わります。

At the next table, a model satellite stayed in orbit around a globe. A loose bread bag caught on the model arm, and an alarm rang. Yuto reached for his bowl. “Is that the dinner bell?”

隣には地球儀を回る人工衛星の模型。袋がアームに引っかかって警報が鳴り、ユウトは椀に手を伸ばします。「夕飯のベル？」

Nozomi tried to push a straw into her soup bag. A drop landed on the tray. An engineer handed her a lid with a tiny cover for the straw. “Our research is about making this easy to open for left-handed and right-handed people. People commonly test it with the hand they usually write with. Try it with the other one. It should move smoothly. The fastener's function is to keep the soup inside until you're ready to drink.” Nozomi closed the lid and turned the bag upside down. None of the contents spilled out, even when she shook it. “That's our quality test in a normal situation,” said the engineer. “What if the person holding it is upside down?” Nozomi held the soup over her head. Yuto ducked. No soup came out.

のぞみがストローを差そうとすると一滴がトレーへ。技術者がストロー用の小さな覆い付きのふたを渡します。左右どちらの利き手でも開けられる研究で「ふだん書く手と反対で試して。なめらかに動くはず。留め具は飲むまで中身を留めるもの」。のぞみは閉じて袋を逆さにし振りますが、こぼれません。「普通なら合格。持つ人が逆さなら？」 のぞみが頭上に掲げ、ユウトが身をかがめても一滴も落ちません。

“Can we heat the soup with the lid on?” she asked. The engineer said to reuse the bowl for heating, then clean it with soap and showed her how to fill the bag through its little cover. Nozomi opened it with her left hand. “So left-handedness doesn't matter,” she said, testing the cover with each hand. Nozomi looked at Yuto's open bowl and reached for a label.

「ふたをしたまま温められる？」 技術者は椀で温めて石けんで洗うこと、小さな口から袋に詰める方法を教えます。のぞみは左右で試し「左利きでも困らないね」。ユウトの椀にラベルを貼ります。

“GROUND USE ONLY,” it said.

「地上専用」。

Yuto watched the warm soup give off steam. “Fine,” he said. “The ground is an excellent place to have supper.”

湯気を見ながらユウトは「いいよ。地上は夕飯にぴったりだ」。
