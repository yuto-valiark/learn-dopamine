ヘンゼルとグレーテル、道しるべをつくる

Hansel, Gretel, and the Route with No Crumbs

お手本音声

A bird landed on a sign in the woods and ate the point of an arrow. The rest of the arrow was made of bread crumbs, too.

森の看板に鳥が止まり、矢印の先を食べました。残りもパンくずです。

“That sign tells people how to get to our bakery,” said Hansel.

「うちのパン屋への道を教えてくれるんだ」とヘンゼル。

“It used to,” said Gretel. The bird ate the middle of the arrow.

「教えてた、ね」とグレーテル。鳥は真ん中も食べます。

The baker was expecting a group of children that afternoon. Kanato, Nozomi, and Yuto compared the two routes. The short trail crossed a bridge. The wide path went around a pond. A grown-up with a heavy bag would probably choose the wide path.

午後に子どもたちが来るので、かなと、のぞみ、ユウトは二つの道を比べます。近道は橋、広い道は池の周り。重い荷物の大人なら広いほう。

At the fork, Kanato marked a tree with a thick, bent branch on his map. He wrote, “Keep the pond on your left.”

分かれ道で太く曲がった枝の木を地図に書き「池を左手に」。

“Which pond?” asked Nozomi. A second pond was hidden by tall grass. It was impossible to see both ponds unless you stood right by the tree. The branch was a good landmark, but the instructions didn't say which pond to keep on the left. A visitor could easily go in the wrong direction.

のぞみが「どっちの池？」 草の奥にもう一つあり、木のすぐそばに立たないと両方見えません。枝はよい目印でも、どちらの池か分からず違う方向へ行けてしまいます。

Gretel and Hansel discussed where to put the new signs. Gretel painted a white arrow at the fork. Hansel put a green sign at the far end of the bridge. It was a second clue. “Make sure that there's a green sign at the end of the bridge,” Kanato wrote. It would confirm that the children were on the right trail.

ヘンゼルとグレーテルは看板の位置を相談。グレーテルが分かれ道に白い矢印、ヘンゼルが橋の先に緑の看板を置きます。「橋の先に緑の看板があるのを確かめて」とかなとが書きます。これで道が合っていると分かります。

By the time Kanato had finished the map, the children had already reached the bakery. “We followed the white arrow,” one of them said.

地図を仕上げるころには、子どもはもう店に到着。「白い矢印についてきたよ」。

The bird pecked at the white arrow by the bakery door. This one wasn't edible. It hopped to the back of the line and chirped.

鳥が店の入り口の白い矢印をつつきます。今度は食べられません。ぴょんと列の最後に並びチュンと鳴きます。

“I wonder what it wants,” said Kanato.

「何が欲しいんだろう」とかなと。

“Another arrow, please,” said Yuto in his best bird voice.

「矢印、おかわりください」とユウトが鳥の声をまねます。
