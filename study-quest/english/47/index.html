ねこがつくった世界会議

The Cat Who Chaired the Meeting

お手本音声

A cat who worked at the theater invited Kanato, Nozomi, and Yuto to a meeting at the park. The invitation said that the chairperson would be absent for the first ten minutes. When they arrived, the cat was asleep on the table. The table was in the middle of the narrow path that led to the playground.

“You're right here,” said Nozomi. “And that's a terrible place for an office. Someone will trip over it.”

“I am officially absent,” the cat said without opening his eyes.

When the meeting started, the cat proposed a public rule: nobody should make noise near his favorite bench. A parent objected. “It would be unfair to punish everyone for playing,” she said. “My children play near that bench.” An older neighbor said he would like a quiet spot to talk with friends. Yuto asked each person to explain what would make the park useful for them.

Before they discussed a solution, a visitor with a pushchair asked if someone could move the cat's table. Three people carried it to the side. The visitor pushed straight through to the playground and the public toilet. The parent, whose anger had been rising, helped the cat move his papers to the new table. Kanato drew two areas on a plan: quiet benches by the trees and open space for games.

“Can we try it before we approve it?” asked the parent. “I'd like my grandchildren to be able to play while I talk to my neighbor.” The cat's main concern had been reducing the noise. Personally, he would have preferred a rule that took effect that afternoon. He looked at the bench near the trees. It was in the sun. “The point is to give everyone some room,” he said. “We don't need a major rule for the whole town.” “One month,” he said. “Then we'll ask for feedback. If people like it, we might broaden the trial to the rest of the park.”

They agreed to the trial. The cat requested a small nameplate on the new bench.

“People may think they aren't allowed to sit there,” said the parent.

The cat considered this. He asked his secretary to type a note on the park website and put it on the seat itself, too: “Please sit here. I like company.” Kanato did so. When he came back the following day, someone had added “The chairperson will be absent from one to two.” The cat was there, of course, asleep all through the official absence. A child left a leaf beside him as a visitor's badge. He wore it all the way home.
