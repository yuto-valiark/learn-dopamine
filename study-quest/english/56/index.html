ユウトの影、面接を受ける

The Shadow That Went to an Interview

お手本音声

On his way to the theater, Yuto noticed that his shadow was missing. Kanato suggested that the light might be coming from directly above. Nozomi looked at the ground. Her own shadow was clearly visible. “Something else must be going on,” she said. A paper had been left where Yuto's shadow usually stood. It said, “I'm applying for a job. Back before sunset. Please don't worry if you get home first.”

劇場へ向かうユウトは影がないことに気づきます。かなとは真上からの光かもと言いますが、のぞみの影ははっきり。「別のことが起きてるはず」。いつもの場所に紙があり「仕事に応募中。日没前に戻る。先に帰っても心配しないで」。

The manager at the theater had received a number of serious applications. One candidate had written, in the style of a very formal speech, “I have supported the same person for years. I am always one step behind him.” Another page said the candidate had no experience of working in a dark room. The manager took this as a sign of honesty and invited Yuto's shadow to an interview.

支配人のもとにはまじめな応募がいくつも。「何年も同じ人を支えていつも一歩後ろ」と堅い文体。「暗い部屋で働いた経験なし」と別頁にあり、正直だと面接へ。

They found the shadow on the stage. It was standing very tall because the light was coming from low down. “It's a trick we use here to make the stage look haunted,” the manager said. The manager asked whether it would be able to work when the main lights were off. “Most of our assistants are human and can carry a lamp,” she said. The shadow said it could work whenever someone turned on a lamp and stood in front of it.

舞台で影は下からの光で背が高く、支配人は「舞台を幽霊が出るみたいに見せる技」。主照明を消しても働けるかと聞き「助手はたいてい人間でランプを持てる」と言います。影は誰かがランプをつけ前に立てば働けると答えます。

“That may be a little difficult during a performance,” she said.

支配人は「上演中は少し難しいかも」。

Nozomi moved a lamp to the side. Kanato blocked part of the light with his hand, and the hand's shadow looked like a bird. Then Yuto's shadow stretched one arm out. The two shapes made a strange combination at first. With one quick movement of its fingers, the shadow made the bird flap a second wing. A child by the door cried out in delight.

のぞみがランプを横へ。かなとの手が光を遮り鳥の形に。ユウトの影が腕を伸ばし指で二枚目の翼を動かすと、戸口の子どもが喜びます。

The manager lowered the lamp. The bird doubled in size. The child stepped closer and asked how they had done it. The manager asked the shadow, “Could you explain what happens when the light comes from a different direction? Surely I can't be the only person who wants to try.” She pulled up a chair for the child.

ランプを下げると鳥が倍になり子どもが方法を質問。支配人は椅子を引き「別の方向の光で何が起こるか説明して。私以外も試したいはず」。

Yuto's shadow accepted the temporary position. Its first performance ended when the lights went out. A small child asked where the assistant had gone.

影は臨時で採用され、初演は消灯で終了。小さな子どもが「助手さんはどこ？」と聞きます。

“He can't be behind me. I think he might be taking his break,” Yuto said. It was the first time he had gone to work with someone who could leave without opening a door. At sunset, the shadow rejoined him. It had added “can disappear at short notice” to its application.

ユウトは「僕の後ろにはいない。休憩かも」。ドアを開けず帰れる相手とは初めて。夕暮れに戻った影は応募書類へ「急でも姿を消せる」と足していました。
