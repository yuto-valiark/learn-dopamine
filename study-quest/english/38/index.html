金太郎と山の落としもの屋

Kintaro's Mountain Service

お手本音声

Kanato and Nozomi met Kintaro on a mountain trail. He was carrying a box of lost property. In the box there were two gloves, a cap, and a bell with no string.

かなととのぞみが山道で金太郎に会います。落とし物箱には手袋二枚、帽子、ひものない鈴。

“You should stay on the trail,” he told them. “The rocks by the stream are slippery. You had better stay away from the edge.”

「沢の岩は滑るから道を外れず、端から離れた方がいいよ」。

A bear came out of the trees. He was carrying something much larger than the box could hold.

木々の間から熊が出てきます。箱にはとても入らない大きさのものを持っています。

“I found this,” he said, putting down a tree stump.

熊は切り株を置き、「これを拾ったよ」と言います。

“Was anyone looking for it?” asked Nozomi. “The villagers in the valley usually put a code on the side of anything they've lost.”

のぞみは「探している人は？ 谷の村では落とし物の横に印があるよ」。

The bear thought for a moment. “I haven't asked the tree.”

熊は「木に聞いてないなあ」。

An insect wriggled out from under the pointy end of the stump. The bear reached for a leaf to catch it. Kintaro stopped him. “Some leaves here contain poison. Put them down. It looks frightened enough already.” The bear set the leaf down and reached for his drinking water instead. Kintaro pointed to the other dead wood among the trees. “This landscape is full of little homes like that one. The insects need the stump. Now that we know about their dependence on it, we'd better put it back.” He pointed to the safe path down to the valley. “Avoid the steep slope. In an emergency, call for help and describe where you are by giving the number from the nearest trail sign. Then the rescue team will know where to look.”

切り株の尖った方から虫がもぞもぞ出ます。葉で捕まえようとする熊を止め「毒のある葉もある。怖がっているよ」と金太郎。熊は葉を置いて水を手に取ります。「枯れ木が虫の家なんだ。虫には切り株が必要だから、戻そう」。谷への安全な道を示し、急な斜面を避け、緊急なら最寄りの道しるべの番号を伝えるように言います。

As soon as the bear put the stump back, a hiker asked them for help. She had lost her map. The last trail sign had been number six. They went back and found the map among the stones under the sign.

熊が切り株を戻すとすぐ、登山者が助けを求めてきます。地図をなくしたのです。最後に見た道しるべは六番でした。みんなで戻ると、道しるべの下の石の間に地図がありました。

At the end of the day, Kintaro changed the rules at his desk. The bear leaned over to read, brushing a twig out of the fur on his neck. Anyone who found something should report where it had been found first. Objects that had never gone anywhere were usually best left alone.

夕方、机で規則を変更します。熊は首の毛の小枝を払い、のぞきます。まず場所を知らせること、元の場所にある物はそのまま。

The bear read the rules and put one paw on the desk. “All right. I'd like to report that I found this desk right here.”

熊は机に前足を置き「この机をここで見つけたと報告します」。

“That's where it belongs,” said Kintaro.

金太郎は「それが元の場所だよ」。

“I'm getting better at this,” said the bear. He left the desk right where it was.

熊は「上手になってきた」と机を動かさず帰ります。
