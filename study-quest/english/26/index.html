浦島太郎と遅刻しない時計

Urashima's Clock

お手本音声

Kanato and Nozomi were looking for shells when a turtle came to the shore. Urashima was on its back. He invited them to the palace under the sea.

かなととのぞみが貝を探していると、亀が浜へ来ます。背中の浦島は海の下の宮殿に招きます。

At the pier, a guidebook promised a series of ten-minute rides. A sign offered a virtual tour for anyone who wanted to stay on land. “Ten minutes by whose clock?” asked Nozomi. The turtle was diving now. Yuto decided to wait on the shore. A canoe drifted past at sunrise while he tried to open a bag of chips. “This adventurer is staying on dry land today. I feel a little sick just watching that boat,” he said.

桟橋の案内本には十分間の船旅。陸に残る人用には仮想ツアーの札。「誰の時計で十分？」とのぞみ。亀はもう潜っています。ユウトは浜に残り、日の出にカヌーが通る間、ポテトチップスの袋を開けようとします。「今日の冒険家は陸にいる。船を見るだけで少し気持ちが悪いよ」

While the others were eating, Kanato looked for a clock. The only clock in the palace had a picture of breakfast at the top and a picture of dinner at the bottom.

みなが食べる間、かなとは時計を探します。唯一の時計の上に朝食、下に夕食の絵。

“What time is it?” he asked a visitor.

「今何時？」とかなとはお客さんに聞きます。

“Almost soup,” she said. “There's a concert after that, although it might start late if the musicians want a second bowl.”

「もうすぐスープ。あとで演奏会だけど、おかわりしたら遅くなるかも」。

Nozomi remembered how long Urashima had been away in the old story. “Can you take us back before our next meal?” she asked the turtle. The turtle agreed. To keep its promise, it took them straight back instead of taking the peaceful route past the sea garden.

のぞみは昔話で浦島が長く留守にしたことを思い出します。「次の食事の前に帰れる？」 亀は約束を守り、海の庭を通る穏やかな道をやめて直帰します。

When they got back to land, Yuto was still opening a bag of chips. They discovered that only five minutes had passed.

陸へ戻るとユウトはまだ袋を開けているところ。五分しか経っていません。

Urashima looked at the turtle. “Can I book another trip? I have time for a five-minute nap.”

「もう一回予約できる？ 五分なら昼寝できる」と浦島。

“Come before soup,” said the turtle.

「スープの前に来てね」と亀。
