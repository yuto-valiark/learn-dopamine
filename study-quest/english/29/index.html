水族館の忘れ物は海を渡る

A Glove Goes to the Aquarium

お手本音声

There is a blue glove on the floor at the aquarium. Kanato takes it to a guide. “Is there a lost property box anywhere?”

“Beside the exit,” she says. “Wait. Where did you find this?”

Kanato points to a tank. A crab is holding up another blue glove. Above it, a notice says volunteers can help feed the animals. Nozomi laughs. “Look. A volunteer.”

Yuto turns over the glove from the floor. There is a note tucked inside. It says, “LET ME OUT.”

“Out of what?” asks Kanato. Yuto holds the glove up to the light. “It's slimy inside. Something just slid down to the thumb.” He hands it back to the guide, who gives it a gentle shake.

The guide turns the glove inside out. A little toy crab, with a piece of fish stuck to its foot, falls out. Then three pieces of fish fall onto a tray. “The person who feeds the animals hides the fish in a rubber glove,” she says. “The last helper writes a little note for every feeding. This one must mean the fish are ready to come out.” She looks at the crab. “Well, your lunch is out. The beauty of this glove is that even a crab can open it.”

A visitor starts to take a photograph. The guide points to the photography guidelines. She gives the visitor a colorful fish cutout to take home. “No flash, please. We're streaming the feeding. There's an option for viewing it without sound.” “If you upload a clip later, I'll show my dad the crab's debut,” she says. At feeding time, the guide carries the tray over to the tank. The crab isn't patient. Before she puts the food down, it pushes the second glove up against the glass.

“Is there another note in that one?” asks Nozomi.

“No,” says the guide. “I think the crab wants you to shake its hand.”
