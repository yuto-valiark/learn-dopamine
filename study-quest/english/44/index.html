おむすびころりん、利用規約を変える

The Rice Ball and the Mouse Agreement

お手本音声

Kanato dropped a rice ball. It rolled into a hole, and a tiny bell rang. Nozomi leaned over the opening. Below them, mice were standing in a bright room around a table set with small plates.

かなとがおむすびを落とすと穴へ転がり、小さな鈴が鳴ります。のぞみがのぞくと皿の並んだ明るい部屋にネズミたち。

“Thank you for your donation,” said a mouse. “According to our rules, we can only accept food that rolls in. That is part of our agreement.”

「寄付ありがとう。転がってきた食べ物だけ受け取る決まりです」。

Yuto offered a sandwich. It would not roll. The mice tried to roll it sideways, but the filling slid out. “I hate watching a good lunch fall apart,” said Yuto. “It's difficult for a sandwich to follow your rules,” said Nozomi.

ユウトのサンドイッチは転がらず、横にすると具が出ます。「お昼がばらばらになるのは嫌だ」「このサンドイッチには決まりを守るのは難しいよ」とのぞみ。

The mice explained why they had made the rule. When people found out about the kitchen, donations began to turn up everywhere. They needed a way to limit what came in. They liked the custom, but they were now turning away useful gifts. Nozomi asked whether a shelf outside the entrance would help. Kanato offered to build it. It was kind of him to offer, the mice said, but they wanted to help with the work.

台所が知られて寄付を制限する決まりを作ったけれど、役立つ品も断っています。入口の外に棚はどうかとのぞみ。かなとが作ると申し出ると、親切だが手伝いたいとネズミ。

They built the shelf that afternoon. Yuto left a new sandwich on it beside a bag of chestnuts. He wrote “CHESTNUTS AND PARSLEY” on the bag so the cook would know what to use for the filling. The cook kept it away from the peanut sauce so people could tell which ingredients went into each dish. Besides the food, someone had put a little sign on the shelf: “Take a plate if you need one.” The mice collected the sandwich and divided it equally. The food on the shelf created a queue. The cook rang the little bell to invite the next mouse over. One mouse offered to distribute the extra food daily. Her first stop was the oldest mouse in the community, who rarely climbed to the entrance and was often lonely at supper. “I cannot bring food here by myself,” she said. “But I can help serve it. Tomorrow I'd like the opportunity to help serve it again.”

午後に棚を作り、ユウトはサンドイッチと袋を置き「栗とパセリ」と書きます。料理係はピーナツのたれと離して材料を分かるようにします。「お皿が必要ならどうぞ」の札も。ネズミは等分にし、列ができると鈴で呼びます。余りを毎日届ける役の最初の相手は、お年寄りのネズミ。入口まで来られず夕食もよく一人です。「食べ物を持ち寄れないけど、配るのは手伝える。明日も配りたい」

After a week, people had left fruit, crackers, and a bag of rice on the shelf. The mice voted to keep it. After the vote, one mouse cut a roast potato into pieces to share. “We can call that a decoration on the shelf until somebody gets hungry,” she said. The next morning the shelf itself rolled down the hole. Someone had put wheels on its legs.

一週間で果物やクラッカー、米が置かれ、棚を続けることに投票します。あるネズミは焼いたジャガイモを切り「誰かがお腹をすかせるまで飾りと言っておこう」。翌朝は車輪をつけた棚ごと穴へ。

“It follows the old rule,” said Kanato.

「古い決まりを守ってる」

“It's also full of food,” said the mouse. “We'd better keep the wheels.”

「しかも食べ物がいっぱい。車輪は残そう」。
