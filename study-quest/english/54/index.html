町で借りた音が家までついて来る

The Sound Library

お手本音声

The newest library in town did not lend books. It lent sounds that brought back memories. One shelf offered the bell of a bicycle. Another held the sound of a factory gate opening at dawn. Kanato wanted a loud sound to surprise the fridge. He chose a sudden drum solo from the hip-hop class, then put it back when he saw a little note: LOUDER EVERY WEEK. Nozomi checked out a recording of festival traffic on a bridge.

町の新しい図書館は本の代わりに記憶を呼び戻す音を貸します。自転車のベル、明け方の工場の門。冷蔵庫を驚かせたいかなとはヒップホップ教室の急なドラム独奏を選んで「毎週もっと大音量」の紙を見て戻します。のぞみは祭りの日の橋の往来を借ります。

The clerk gave Yuto a file whose label said SILENCE. At home, he turned up the speaker and listened. After twelve seconds there was the sound of someone pouring tea.

係員がユウトに「無音」を渡します。家で音量を上げると十二秒後にお茶を注ぐ音。

“That's my kind of silence,” he said.

「こういう静けさが好き」。

The next day, a woman came to the library to ask whether she could borrow the sound of a train leaving the old station. Her husband had worked there. The original station was gone, and she wanted to hear its bell one more time. The clerk looked up the recording, but it had not been included in the public collection. Voices could be heard in the background, and the people whose voices were recorded had never been asked for permission.

翌日、夫が働いた旧駅の列車の音を借りたい女性。もう駅はなく、鐘をまた聞きたい。録音には背景の声があり、許可を取っていないので公開資料に入っていません。

The clerk opened the sound archive. “Someone's interview starts just after the bell. We can't publish that interview. I could dub new voices over it, but the dubbing might cover the bell, too. Listen to the first three seconds. You can hear it ring before anyone speaks.” He played the chime on a loop so the visitor could hear its tempo and compare it with the tempo of a drum. She tapped it out on the counter with one finger. Each time it stopped just before the private conversation began. “Can you put those few seconds in the public playlist?” she asked. “That's the part I remember. We don't need a translation for a bell.”

係員が資料を開き「鐘の直後に取材があり公開できない。吹き替えでも鐘を覆うかも。最初の三秒、誰かが話す前に鐘が鳴る」。ループでテンポを聞き、彼女は指で机をたたきます。毎回私的な会話の手前で止まります。「この数秒を公開リストに。思い出すのはここ。鐘に翻訳はいらない」。

The clerk saved the short extract and added a brief explanation of where it had been recorded. Now anybody could find the bell in the catalog. A week later, the woman brought him a recording of her own. Her grandson had rung a small bell in the same rhythm. He wanted to donate the recording, including the sound of his own laughter at the end.

係員は抜粋に録音場所を添え、目録で探せるようにします。一週間後、孫が同じリズムで小さな鐘を鳴らし、笑い声も含め寄付したいと言う録音を持ってきます。

The clerk asked what label he should put on the new sound.

係員は、この新しい音にどんな名前をつけたらいいかと聞きます。

“Two generations,” said the woman.

「二世代」と女性。

Yuto went to exchange his file of silence. They offered him one labeled TEA. It was exactly the same file. He decided to keep both.

ユウトは無音を交換しようとし「お茶」を渡されます。まったく同じファイル。両方持つことにします。
