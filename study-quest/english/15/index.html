ボブという名の植物

A Plant Named Bob

お手本音声

Nozomi has had a plant for two months. Its name is Bob. It has been on the kitchen table since April.

“It hasn't grown at all,” says Kanato.

“I know,” says Nozomi. “But it has looked green for weeks.”

Outside, Nozomi finds a bur on Dad's coat. Its little hooks hold on to the cloth. She and Kanato try to imitate the hooks. They want to invent a fastener for a school project. “That's biomimetic design,” says Nozomi. “We're copying a feature of the plant.” She writes biomimetics above the drawing of the hooks.

“Let's look at the leaves and seeds of different plants,” says Nozomi. “We can put a flowering plant and a crop on our chart.”

Kanato brings Bob outside to look at his leaves. He tries to bend one. All the leaves move together. He turns the pot around.

“It seems to be made of plastic,” says Dad.

“That explains it. It has never asked for water,” says Kanato. He looks at the chart. “What should we write?”

Nozomi writes “Stays green. Never grows.” Then she puts Bob back on the table.
