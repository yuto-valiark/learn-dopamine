ボブという名の植物

A Plant Named Bob

お手本音声

Nozomi has had a plant named Bob for two months.

It has been on the kitchen table since April.

Kanato looks at it. “It hasn’t grown at all.”

“I know,” says Nozomi. “But it has looked green for weeks.”

Dad looks at the pot. “It’s made of plastic.”

“That explains why it has never asked for water,” says Kanato.
