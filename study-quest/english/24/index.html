シンデレラ、靴より先に帰る

Cinderella Leaves Her Shoes On

お手本音声

Cinderella invites Nozomi to the palace dance. “We must leave before midnight,” she says. “I promised to bring back the coach.”

“Do your glass slippers disappear at midnight, too?” asks Kanato.

“I borrowed them from a shop. They're ordinary glass. I must not lose them.”

The coach arrives in the daytime, just before sunset. The golden doors, usually closed at this hour on a weekday, are wide open for the court dance. A few men and women from the orchestra are waiting by the gate. Near the entrance, a performer in a wedding costume is looking for an expensive, delicate silver necklace. Cinderella spots it on the floor. “Is there a desk for lost things?” Cinderella asks. He shakes his head. She checks the straps on her glass slippers one more time.

At eleven fifty, the music is still playing. Just before the orchestra finishes its final piece, Yuto holds the door while Cinderella takes two big steps across the hall. Her shoes are noisy on the stone floor. After the coach arrives at the gate, she lifts her feet to get inside.

The prince runs after her. He is carrying a slipper. It fits nobody in the coach.

“That's mine,” says Yuto. “It's for the bath.”

Cinderella checks both feet. After she returns the coach, she sends the prince a note suggesting a place for lost things. The desk opens the next morning, and Yuto is first in line. He is still wearing the other bath slipper.
