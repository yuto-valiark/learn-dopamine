なくしたくつ下博物館

The Museum of Lost Socks

お手本音声

Nozomi enjoys finding lost things.

Kanato likes looking for socks, but he never finds two that match.

They decide to open a small museum.

After they finish making the signs, their first visitor comes in.

It is Dad. He looks at a red sock.

“Great,” he says. “Now I can finish getting dressed.”
