Puss in Boots Applies for a Job

お手本音声

Puss in Boots needed a job. He wanted to work at the town theater, where he could sit near a warm stage. His application said that he had been captain of the Royal Department of Naps.

“Is that a real title?” asked Nozomi.

“It was real while I was asleep,” said the cat.

Kanato asked him to list skills he was honestly proud of. Puss could catch a falling cup, notice a mouse behind a wall, and walk across a table without moving a plate. Yuto suggested writing those skills down. He gave the cat a pencil to use, but the cat inspected it for several seconds before knocking it onto the floor.

An actor from overseas passed them on her way to collect a costume. Looking down at the stage, she asked her interpreter to find out whether anyone had seen a crown clip. She knew where the clip went in the anime version, but the real costume looked different. The usually easygoing actor was due to perform in ten minutes and could hardly stand still. She had to act the part of a queen in a musical based on a manga. The crown wouldn't stay on without its clip. “It's a precious family crown,” she said, “or it was until it met this theater's floor.” As she carried the costume past a piece of scenery, one of its loose metal buttons fell through a gap in the stage. The cat watched where it went. So did the manager.

“I'm looking for an applicant who can find what falls through these boards,” said the manager. “See that gap? I dropped a key there.” Puss disappeared into the gap. He came back almost immediately with the key, the actor's button, the crown clip, and the manager's missing ring. “That's impressive,” said the manager. “You're a talented applicant.” “Only the key was necessary,” she said. “You'll develop a real reputation if you keep doing this.” “How did you find everything so quickly?”

“You're hired,” she said. “One position, one lucky applicant,” she said, putting on her ring. “Could you stay to collect anything else that falls through?”

Puss wanted to add that he had found a crown once. He looked at Nozomi, crossed out the Royal Department, and wrote “Good at finding things.”

Under that, in smaller letters, he added “Also available for naps.”
