明日の遠足が二つある

Two Trips Tomorrow

お手本音声

Kanato is going to visit the zoo tomorrow. Nozomi is going to visit Grandma. They have only one bicycle.

“Then I'll walk,” says Nozomi.

That evening Grandma calls. “I can drive you both. It's a holiday tomorrow. I'm excited. We're going to spend a day somewhere new.”

“Where will you go?” asks Nozomi. “Will we need a swimsuit or sandals?”

“No. I'm going to the zoo.” Grandma laughs. “Bring sunscreen. The elephants will be outside all day. We will be outside, too.”

Kanato looks at the bicycle. “Great. You'll stay home tomorrow.”
