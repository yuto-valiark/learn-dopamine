第8章

明日の遠足が二つある

Two Trips Tomorrow

お手本音声

Kanato is going to visit the zoo tomorrow.

Nozomi is going to visit Grandma.

“We have only one bicycle,” says Kanato.

“Then I’ll walk,” says Nozomi.

Grandma calls. “I’m going to the zoo, too!”

“Great,” says Kanato. “I’ll ask the bicycle to stay home.”
