橋のトロールは休けい中

The Troll Takes a Break

お手本音声

Three goats are waiting by a bridge. A troll is sitting under it.

“Why aren't you crossing?” asks Nozomi.

“The troll is reading,” says a small goat.

“What are you reading?” asks Kanato. A scary detective novel is beside the troll. Its title is The Mysterious Lantern, but he is holding a different book.

The small goat is scared, but he steps onto the bridge. His bell rings.

“Where are you going?” asks the troll. The goat stops.

The troll closes his book. Kanato reads the title: How to Be Nice to Goats.

“Give me a minute,” says the troll. “I'm still at the beginning.”
