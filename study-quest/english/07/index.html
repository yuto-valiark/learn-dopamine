橋のトロールは休けい中

The Troll Takes a Break

お手本音声

Three goats are waiting by a bridge.

A troll is sitting under it.

“Why aren’t you crossing?” asks Nozomi.

“The troll is reading a book,” says the smallest goat.

Kanato looks down. “What are you reading?”

“A book called How to Be Nice to Goats,” says the troll. “Give me a minute.”
