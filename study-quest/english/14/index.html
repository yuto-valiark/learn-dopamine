The Letter That Came Back

お手本音声

“Have you ever sent a letter to yourself?” Nozomi asks.

“No,” says Kanato. “But I have just sent one to the fridge.”

Nozomi looks at the fridge. “It has already sent it back.”

The letter is on the table again.

“It says, ‘I have read your letter,’” says Nozomi.

“And at the bottom it says, ‘Please send pudding instead.’”
