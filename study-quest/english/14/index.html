The Letter That Came Back

お手本音声

“Have you ever sent a letter to yourself?” Nozomi asks.

“No,” says Kanato. “But I have just sent one to the fridge.”

Kanato is writing to a friend in a foreign country. She wants to hear about a tradition in his town. He has written a short poem about the food at a cultural festival. It's a big part of the town's culture. At the end he asks, “Would you like to try it?”

Before Kanato sends his friend the e-mail, he leaves a copy on the fridge door. He hopes the fridge will respond. It has always shown interest in food, even when Kanato puts photos of it on his blog.

“Did you ask for a reply?” Nozomi asks. Then she looks at the table. “Oh. It has already sent one.”

The letter is on the table again. “It says, ‘I have read your letter,’” says Nozomi. “And at the bottom it says, ‘Please send pudding instead.’”
