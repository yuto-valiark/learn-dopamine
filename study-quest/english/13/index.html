ケーキは食べられました

The Cake Was Eaten

お手本音声

A cake was made for the school party.

It was put on a table near the door.

When the party started, half the cake was gone.

“It was eaten by someone,” said Kanato.

Nozomi looked at the cake. “It was cut into two pieces.”

“The other half is for the teachers,” she added. “I hope.”
