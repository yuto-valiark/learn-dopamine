ケーキは食べられました

The Cake Was Eaten

お手本音声

A cake was made for the school's anniversary party. It was put on a table near the cafeteria door. The students were raising money for charity. Pink balloons were tied to a vase on the table. Kanato put aside some paper cups to recycle instead of throwing them in the trash. Kanato went to get the candles. When he got back, half the cake was gone.

“It was eaten by someone,” he said.

Nozomi looked at the knife. “It was cut into two pieces,” she said. “The pink half was meant for the teachers.”

A cheerful teacher came in with an empty plate. A little pink cream was left on the edge.

“Was the pink half for us?” she asked.

“Yes, after the song,” Nozomi added. “There's still plenty for the children.”

The teacher put down her plate. “I was going to tell a joke. Someone couldn't wait for dessert. Well, that's the joke.” She started the song.
