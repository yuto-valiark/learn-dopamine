桃太郎とおやつチーム

Momotaro’s Snack Team

お手本音声

Momotaro has three rice dumplings.

Kanato and Nozomi meet him by the river.

“I like rice dumplings,” says Kanato.

A dog comes along. It likes rice dumplings, too.

Momotaro gives each of them one dumpling.

“Wait,” says Nozomi. “Now our leader has no snack!”
