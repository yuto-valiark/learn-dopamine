桃太郎とおやつチーム

Momotaro’s Snack Team

お手本音声

Momotaro has three rice dumplings. He meets Kanato and Nozomi by the river.

“I like rice dumplings,” says Kanato.

A dog comes along. It likes rice dumplings, too. Their ship is still there. They have time for a snack.

Momotaro gives each of them one dumpling. The dog eats its dumpling first.

“Wait,” says Nozomi. “Now our leader has no snack!”

The dog looks at the ship. “I like lunch, too.”
