第18章

リモコンはどこ？

Where Is the Remote?

お手本音声

Kanato asks, “Do you know where the remote is?”

“I don’t know,” says Nozomi. “Do you remember when you used it?”

Kanato looks under the sofa. There is only a sock.

The fridge asks, “Do you know why I’m so cold?”

Nozomi opens the fridge and finds the remote.

“I don’t know why it’s here,” she says, “but it needs a blanket.”
