桃太郎と物語の最後の席

Momotaro and One More Chair

お手本音声

Everyone who had helped Momotaro choose a new ending for his story was invited to his party. The invitation asked each guest to bring one thing they had learned. Kanato brought a map with useful landmarks. Nozomi brought a card that said, “Check how many guests there are before sharing the dumplings.” Yuto brought folding chairs.

桃太郎の結末を手伝った人は全員招待され、学んだものを一つ持ち寄ります。かなとは目印の地図、のぞみは「だんごを分ける前に人数を数える」カード、ユウトは折りたたみ椅子。

The whole community was welcome. They had chosen a picnic theme, with paper decorations made from leftover school supplies. They gathered by the river for a picnic. Kaguya arrived early because she had checked the moon bus schedule. The wolf found the right address on his first attempt. A mouse brought a sandwich on a tray and asked whether it had to roll. Momotaro offered him a plate.

町のみなを歓迎し学校の余りで紙飾りを作って川辺でピクニック。かぐや姫はバス時刻を調べ早く到着。狼は一度で住所へ。ネズミがサンドイッチを転がすべきか聞くと桃太郎は皿を渡します。

Momotaro had remembered to count the dumplings. He had even saved one for himself. The chairs were another matter. Every seat was taken, and someone was standing at the edge of the group, near the entrance. It was the smallest of the three pigs. He had offered his place to a visitor who could not reach the row near the back.

桃太郎はだんごの数を忘れず数えて、自分にも一つ残していました。ところが、椅子のほうはそううまくいきません。席は全部ふさがっていて、入り口近くの輪の端に、立っている人がいます。三匹のうちいちばん小さな子ぶたです。奥の列まで行けないお客さんに席をゆずったのでした。

“If we had another chair, there would be room for everybody,” said Kanato.

「もう一脚あれば全員座れる」とかなと。

“We have one at home,” said the fridge. It had been standing in the shade with a tray of cold drinks. Yuto asked whether it meant the chair they had put by the school gate. The fridge nodded. Yuto went back for it, and Nozomi moved the other chairs so the new place would fit into the circle.

飲み物のトレーと日陰にいた冷蔵庫が「家に一脚」。ユウトが校門に置いた椅子か聞くとうなずき、取りに戻ります。のぞみは輪に席を作ります。

When the sun went down, Momotaro asked the guests what kind of story they wanted to hear next. The wolf wanted a mystery in which nobody went to the wrong house. Kaguya wanted a story set on Earth. The smallest pig wanted a house with a door that everyone could open.

日暮れに桃太郎が次の話を聞きます。狼は誰も家を間違えないなぞ、かぐや姫は地球、子ぶたは誰でも開けられる扉の家。

Kanato wrote down their ideas. On Kaguya's line he added, “A story about friendship, a little sadness, and a castle on Earth.” He left one more line blank. “You can choose what we write first,” he said, sliding the paper toward the smallest pig.

かなとは友情と少しの悲しさと地球の城を姫の行に書き、空欄を残し子ぶたに最初を選んでもらいます。

“Something to share,” said the pig.

「何かを分けること」と子ぶた。

The fridge opened its door. There was one pudding in the back. It had kept it cold all afternoon without saying a word.

冷蔵庫がドアを開けます。奥には、一言も言わず午後じゅう冷やしていたプリンが一つあります。

“Would anyone like to share?” it asked. “It would be a pleasure,” said the wolf, who had brought three spoons.

「誰か、分けて食べたい人はいる？」と冷蔵庫が聞きます。スプーン三本の狼は「ぜひ」。

There were a lot of hands in the air. The fridge looked at them for a moment. Then it asked Nozomi for the card about counting. This time, it had an idea for the ending and wanted to get it right.

手がたくさん上がります。冷蔵庫は眺めて、のぞみに数えるカードを頼みます。今度は結末の案があり、うまくやりたいのです。
