犬が社長の面接

The Dog Who Ran the Office

お手本音声

A sign in the window said, “Weekend job. Must be polite. Apply inside.” Nozomi and Kanato went in.

“Whose office is this?” asked Kanato.

“Mine,” said a brown dog. He wore a tie and had six sticky notes around a bone. “I'm the manager. Who can answer the company phone? I need someone who can communicate clearly as well as keep a basic record on this little blackboard.”

Nozomi picked up the black receiver. “This one?”

“The red one. The black one calls the owner of the shop next door. He sells sausages. I try to make only one mistake a day.”

Nozomi put down the black receiver. “What kind of business is this? Do we need to bring tools?” The dog pushed a pump across the table. He slid a dictionary over, too. “We use that to check the spelling of the customers' names.” “No. I'll provide training. And the shop next door gives employees a discount. On sausage. That's the important part.”

The dog gave them a schedule. They would help people repair bikes on Saturday and give directions to the airport on Sunday. “Last weekend I told people to turn left when they could see an airplane,” the dog said. “So that's the one I need help with.” Then the interview turned to their experience.

“Have you ever worked with a dog?” he asked.

“Yes,” said Kanato. “I helped Momotaro.”

“No,” said Nozomi at the same time. “We did all the work. The dog ate the pay.”

The manager wrote both answers down. Then he moved the bone a little closer to himself.
