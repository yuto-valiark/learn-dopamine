第17章

めがねをかけた犬

The Dog Wearing Glasses

お手本音声

Nozomi sees a dog wearing glasses in the park.

Kanato picks up a book left on a bench.

The dog comes over and looks at the book.

“Is this yours?” asks Kanato.

The dog points to a picture drawn on the cover.

It is a picture of a dog teaching English.
