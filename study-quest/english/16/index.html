何も開かないカギ

A Key That Opens Nothing

お手本音声

Kanato finds a key that has a star on it.

Nozomi knows a man who makes keys.

He looks at it. “Try the box that is behind you.”

Kanato tries the key, but the box is already open.

Inside is a note that says, “This key opens nothing.”

“It opened a mystery,” says Nozomi.
