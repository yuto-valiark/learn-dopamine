かぐや姫、地球に延長申請

Kaguya Asks for One More Night

お手本音声

Princess Kaguya is going to return to the moon tonight. Nozomi helps her pack. “Do you really have to take the moon bus? I thought you were going by plane.” Kanato asks what he should send her for her birthday.

かぐや姫は今夜月へ帰ります。荷造りを手伝うのぞみは「月バス？ 飛行機かと思った」。かなとが誕生日に何を送ろうかと聞きます。

“A postcard,” she says. “Pictures of Earth are hard to find up there.”

「絵はがき。向こうでは地球の写真がなかなか手に入らないの」。

When the moon comes over the bamboo grove, an empty bus arrives. Its driver gives Yuto a slip of paper. The moon traffic is slow. They should expect a two-hour delay.

竹やぶに月が出たら空のバスが来ます。運転手がユウトに紙を渡します。月の道路が混み、二時間遅れ。

“Then I'll stay until the next bus,” says Kaguya.

「じゃあ次のバスまでいるね」とかぐや姫。

The driver shows them a photo of the moon road. To the west, a cloud of pale gray dust hides the bus ahead. “The engineering team gave this modern bus more powerful headlights,” he says, “but look what happens when it drives over that dusty road.” “If we increase our speed, the wheels kick up even more dust. It makes the next bus look like a faraway star.” Kaguya holds the photo in the moonlight. She can just make out two red lights in the dust. “The next bus is probably only a few kilometers from the stop, but it might as well be a million. If we could clear the dust, we could get home faster.”

運転手が月の道路の写真を見せます。西では灰色の砂ぼこりに前のバスが隠れています。「技術者がヘッドライトを強くしましたが、道がほこりっぽくて。速くするとさらに巻き上がり、前のバスが遠い星に見えるんです」 かぐや姫が月明かりで写真を見ると赤い灯が二つ。「次のバスは数キロ先なのに百万キロみたい。ほこりを払えれば早く帰れるのに」

Before the next bus comes, the driver asks whether anyone has something made of bamboo. “We don't grow it on the moon,” he says.

次が来る前、運転手は竹でできた物を尋ねます。「月では竹が育たないので」

Kanato offers him a small fan. Yuto offers him a whole bamboo broom. The driver picks the broom and ties it to the roof.

かなとは小さなうちわを、ユウトは竹ぼうきを差し出します。運転手はほうきを選んで屋根に結びます。

“Are you going to sweep the moon?” asks Nozomi.

「月を掃くの？」とのぞみ。

“I'm going to clear the road,” says the driver. He points to the mouth of a tunnel on his map. “If the broom clears the dust, we can continue through here.”

「道を掃くよ」 運転手は地図のトンネル入り口を指します。「ほこりが払えたらここを通れます」
