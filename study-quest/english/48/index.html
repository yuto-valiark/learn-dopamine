かさじぞうと玄関の天気観測所

Straw Hats and the Winter Record

お手本音声

It was the first heavy snow of the winter. Kanato and Nozomi stopped next to six stone statues. Someone had cleared the snow from the tops of their heads, but the snow had already covered their shoulders again. A woman wearing a thin coat was brushing off the sixth one with her sleeve. She had left her comfortable kitchen before her tea was ready. A neighbor saw her shiver and came out to offer her a hug and a dry scarf. “You don't want to be in bed with a fever again,” she said.

冬の初めての大雪。六体の石のお地蔵さまの頭は払ってありますが、肩はまた覆われています。薄いコートの女性が六体目を袖で払います。お茶ができる前に暖かい台所から出てきたので、震えるのを見た近所の人が抱きしめ乾いたマフラーを渡します。「また熱で寝込まないで」

“If I had six spare hats, I'd give them all one,” she said. “I have only this cloth.”

「帽子が六つあればみんなに。でもこの布だけ」。

Yuto asked if she lived nearby. She pointed at a house just beyond the bridge. Her neighbor normally brought in the outdoor chairs, but he had been away for several days. “If the path were frozen, we couldn't carry the chairs back safely,” the woman said. She did not want anyone to slip. Kanato and Yuto moved the chairs while Nozomi put the cloth over the smallest statue's head.

ユウトが家を聞くと橋の先を示します。いつも庭の椅子を入れる隣人は数日留守。「道が凍っていれば安全に運べない」。ユウトとかなとが椅子を運び、のぞみは一番小さなお地蔵さまに布をかけます。

The next morning, a box appeared at the house. It held six straw hats. There was enough room for one more thing, so it also contained a warm coat in the woman's size. Nobody knew who had left it. A note asked the family to send a report of the depth of the snow in return.

翌朝、その家に箱が届きます。中にはわらの帽子が六つ。もう一つ入る余裕があったので、女性にぴったりの暖かいコートも入っていました。誰が置いていったのかは分かりません。メモには、お返しに雪の深さを知らせてくださいと書いてありました。

Nozomi put a measuring stick on flat ground away from the wall. Each day the woman recorded the depth at the same hour. She hung a sign above the stick: “Measuring the village's height.” Once the snow stopped, the woman took the six hats to the statues. Another family offered to record the temperature while she was out. The information helped neighbors decide whether to clear the path before dark.

のぞみは壁から離れた平らな地面に物差しを立て、女性は同じ時刻に記録します。札は「村の身長を測定中」。雪が止むと帽子を届け、別の家族が気温を測ります。暗くなる前に雪かきする判断に役立ちます。

One afternoon the measuring stick showed there was less snow. A neighbor and her grandchild came to check the stick. At first the child thought the dirty patch was sand, but it was where water from the roof had frozen. The neighbor asked the child to go around it. The child pointed to the path. “Now that the ground's wet, it might freeze again,” the neighbor said. There was ice underneath. Together they cleared the frozen patch before the children came home.

雪が減った午後、近所の人と孫が来ます。砂のような色の箇所は屋根からの水が凍ったもの。回り道させて、ぬれた地面はまた凍るかもと話します。下の氷を、子どもが帰る前に片づけます。

When the ground was clear again, the woman put on the new coat. “I promised the neighbors I'd take better care of my health,” she said. Then she took photographs of all six statues. Their straw hats were crooked, and three birds had made use of the sixth one. She pulled the coat close and waved.

道がきれいになると新しいコートを着て「健康に気をつけると約束したから」。六体を撮影すると帽子は曲がり、六つ目には鳥が三羽。コートを引き寄せ手を振ります。

“If the person who sent this were here,” she said, “I'd offer them tea.”

「届けた人がここにいたら、お茶を出すのに」。

A small patch of snow fell off the nearest statue's shoulder, just as if it had stepped closer.

いちばん近い石の肩の雪が、近づいてきたかのように少し落ちました。
