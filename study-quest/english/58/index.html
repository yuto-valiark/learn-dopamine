透明マントの学校説明会

A Guide to Being Seen

お手本音声

For the school's open day, Kanato was given a cloak left behind by a visiting magician. The instructions explained that anyone wearing it would become invisible. Kanato put it on and picked up a guide sign. At the front entrance, families saw a board marked SCHOOL TOUR floating through the air.

学校公開日、かなとは手品師の置き忘れた透明マントを渡されます。説明書どおり着て「学校見学」の札を持つと、家族に札だけ浮いて見えます。

“The sign is very helpful,” a visitor said. “Is there a person attached to it?”

「役に立つけど、人も付いてますか？」

Kanato tried to explain the route without taking off the cloak. At the first crossing, he pointed left. The families kept walking straight. A visitor who had only recently moved to town called, “I don't know the building. What should I look for?” “Ah,” he said. Being invisible let him stand right in front of the map, but it also made him forget that no one could see his hand.

かなとは着たまま案内し最初の角で左を指しますが、家族はまっすぐ。引っ越してきた客が「何を目印に？」 透明だと地図の前に立てても、手が見えないことを忘れさせます。

Back at reception, Yuto connected his tablet to the screen. Nozomi enlarged the map and replaced three little arrows with a picture of the big clock. The letters read TURN LEFT AT THE CLOCK. “Can we translate that phrase for the next group?” asked Yuto. A parent who worked as a translator showed him how to say it in a friendly tone. They checked it face-to-face with a visiting learner. The translator pointed to the map. “If a direction seems too complex, ask the visitor to show you what it says.” “Try it on me. Pretend I have no knowledge of the building at all.” Yuto printed an extra copy and drew an arrow by the clock. Nozomi showed the map to the next visitor and asked her to find that spot on the screen.

受付でユウトがタブレットを画面につなぎ、のぞみは地図を拡大し小矢印三つを大時計の絵に変更。「時計で左へ」。通訳の保護者が訳と優しい言い方を教え、来訪中の学習者と対面で確認します。「難しければどこを指すか示してもらって。私を建物を知らない人として練習して」。ユウトは予備を印刷し時計の横に矢印を描き、のぞみは次のお客さんに地図を見せ、その場所を画面で探してもらいます。

The visitor pointed to the clock right away. Kanato took off the cloak. “Ready to try it for real?” he asked.

お客さんはすぐに時計を指します。かなとは脱いで「実際にやってみる？」。

For the afternoon sightseeing tour, Kanato was visible again. Yuto put on the cloak. When a child dropped a pencil, he picked it up. It had rolled past the restroom and was about to fall down a step. His first successful bit of magic went by almost unnoticed. From the child's point of view, the pencil just floated back into her hand. “Awesome,” she said.

午後の見学ツアーではかなとが見え、ユウトがマントを着ます。鉛筆がトイレ前を転がり段差から落ちそうなとき拾い、透明になった最初の成功はほとんど気づかれません。子どもには、鉛筆がふわっと浮いて手に戻ったように見えます。「すごい」と言いました。

“Was that the magician?” the child asked.

「今の、手品師さん？」と子どもは聞きます。

“An assistant,” said Kanato.

「助手だよ」とかなと。

On the way out, everyone praised the map. The floating sign had made quite an impression as well, but only two people had managed to follow it.

帰りに地図が好評。浮く札も印象的でしたが、従って進めたのは二人だけでした。
