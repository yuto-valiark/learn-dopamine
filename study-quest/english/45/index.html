雨の翌日、高い所へ

The Morning the River Kept Rising

お手本音声

It had rained all night. In the morning, Kanato saw a picture of the river in a group chat from a classmate. The water in the picture looked low. He was about to tell a friend it was safe to use the path by the bank when Nozomi noticed the long shadow of a tree.

“Find out when that picture was taken,” she said.

The message below the picture gave yesterday's date. The news, however, had changed overnight. The latest message gave a new flood warning for their region. Flooding could occur along the river. Yuto had saved the town's official map beforehand. A warning had been issued that morning, and the water level had been rising since six. He sent both children the map and asked them to stay on higher roads.

A neighbor was preparing to go to a shelter with her child. Nozomi helped her choose a route that avoided the riverside path. The neighbor asked Kanato to send a clear message to their street group. Someone in the group doubted that the warning was true, so Yuto drew a line on the map to illustrate exactly where the road was closed. “The law keeps people off the flooded road while it's closed,” he said. “Go around the block. That route is on higher ground.” Kanato wrote down the specific streets named in the latest warning and linked to its source. Then he added the shelter's address and asked people to follow the town's instructions.

The neighbor reached the shelter by the higher route. She sent a short reply. “We're inside now. By the time we got here, our street had been closed. A local non-governmental organization and the fire station brought water and other resources. Someone who slipped on the path is already getting medical help. I wish everyone got the warning as soon as we did.” Kanato watched a new message appear. Someone had shared the old picture again. This time, he sent the link to the latest flood warning.

At their house on the hill, Yuto brought a box labeled IMPORTANT DOCUMENTS to the kitchen table. Nozomi asked whether he had remembered the insurance papers. He opened the box and checked.

“Those are upstairs,” he said. “This box has flashlights, spare batteries, and the family pudding recipe.”

He handed her a flashlight and went upstairs for the papers.
