からっぽの箱

The Empty Box

お手本音声

Nozomi finds a box under the table.

“Do you have a pet in there?” asks Kanato.

“No, I don't. Does this box make a sound?”

Kanato puts his ear next to it. “It doesn't make a sound.”

Nozomi opens it. It is empty. She draws a star-shaped door on the lid with a marker. She uses wrapping paper for the door.

“Does it need a door?” asks Kanato.

“Yes,” says Nozomi. She puts her homework inside. “My homework needs a quiet place.”
