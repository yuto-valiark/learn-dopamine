からっぽの箱

The Empty Box

お手本音声

Nozomi finds a box under the table.

“Do you have a pet in there?” asks Kanato.

“No, I don’t. Does this box make a sound?”

Kanato puts his ear next to it. “It doesn’t make a sound.”

Nozomi opens it. There is nothing inside.

“Good,” she says. “It’s a quiet place for my homework.”
