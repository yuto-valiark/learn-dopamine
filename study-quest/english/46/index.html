眠れる森の姫、起きている

The Princess Who Had Been Waiting

お手本音声

A sign outside the sleeping castle said, “Visitors must wait for the prince.” Nozomi took a closer look. Someone had added a second line in pencil: “How long have you been waiting? A century?”

眠る城の外に「王子をお待ちください」の札。鉛筆の追記は「どれほど待ちましたか？ 百年？」。

“About seven minutes,” Kanato replied. Yuto was trying to find an entrance through the thick wall of thorns. They had just heard a deep sound that seemed to come from every window.

「七分くらい」とかなと。ユウトはとげの壁の入り口を探します。どの窓からも低い音。

“That must be the whole castle snoring,” said Kanato.

「きっと城じゅうのいびきだよ」とかなとは言います。

A woman in gardening gloves stepped through a side gate carrying a pot of roses. She introduced herself as the princess. “The gardener and I have been waiting for someone to fix that noise. I get a headache if I try to relax in the study. Are you here about the roses, too?” She set the pot beside a sign that read THURSDAY, GARDENING ADVICE TODAY.

手袋とバラの鉢の女性が脇門に現れ、姫と名乗ります。「庭師と音を直す人をずっと待っていて、書斎では頭痛がするの。バラの相談？」 鉢を「木曜、園芸相談」の札の横へ。

She showed them a record of when the sound had started. It happened whenever the wind blew from the north. The princess had watched a gold decoration shake above the entrance but heard the sound coming from below it. Very little noise came from the rooms once the windows were closed. Nozomi asked what lay behind the wall. The princess said there was an old metal pipe nobody used any more. They stood by it and heard the next gust echo inside it. Yuto reported the fault to the castle caretaker, who replaced the loose cover.

音が始まった時の記録を見せます。北風のたびに飾りが揺れ、音はその下。窓を閉めた部屋からはほとんど音がしません。壁の奥には使わない金属管があり、風が吹くと響きます。ユウトが管理人に報告して緩いふたを交換します。

They waited for another gust. The princess went back to watering her roses. The flowers bent in the wind, but the castle stayed quiet. “I can't believe it was only a loose cover,” said the gardener. “It's refreshing to hear the wind by itself.” The princess wrote one word in her record: FIXED.

次の風でバラは曲がっても城は静か。「緩いふただけだったなんて。風だけ聞けるのは気持ちいい」と庭師。姫は「修理済み」と書きます。

Someone finally rang the main bell. It was a prince with a bouquet.

ようやく正面のベルが鳴ります。花束を持った王子です。

“I'm here to wake the princess,” he said.

「お姫さまを起こしに来ました」と王子は言います。

“She's been awake since Tuesday,” said Kanato. The prince looked at his appointment card. “That would explain why my Friday appointment says 'roses.'”

かなとが「火曜から起きてるよ」と言い、王子は「だから金曜の予約がバラなのか」。

The prince looked at the roses in the pot, then at his own bouquet. “In my opinion, mine should be taller,” he said. “They've been producing smaller flowers for years.” “This variety used to do well, but lately I can't find a watering schedule that will satisfy it. Could you recommend something?” The princess picked up the pot to show him its soil. She drew a circle around the stem so he could see where the water would hit the soil rather than the leaves. Kanato added a third line to the sign by the entrance: “If you can hear snoring, please report it to maintenance.”

鉢と自分の花束を見て「もっと高く育つはず。何年も花が小さくなっていて、水やりが合わない。助言を？」 姫は土を見せ、葉でなく土に水が当たる場所を示します。かなとは札に「いびきが聞こえたら管理人へ」と足しました。
