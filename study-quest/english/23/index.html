スープを売りたい自動販売機

The Machine That Sells Soup

お手本音声

At the station, Kanato finds a machine with one choice: onion soup. He almost tries a coin from a trip abroad. “This one says one dollar,” says Nozomi. He puts in a hundred-yen coin instead. “How much soup does it serve?”

駅でかなとはオニオンスープ一択の自販機を見つけます。海外旅行の硬貨を入れかけ「一ドルって書いてあるよ」とのぞみに言われ、百円玉にします。「どのくらい出る？」

“One cup,” says Nozomi. “How many cups do you want?”

「一杯。何杯ほしい？」とのぞみは聞きます。

“None,” says Yuto. “I came for tea.”

「いらない。お茶を買いに来た」とユウト。

The machine makes a sad noise. Its screen says, “Soup is a blanket you can drink.”

自販機が悲しそうな音を出します。画面には「スープは飲める毛布です」。

Yuto turns toward the café counter. “Paying by card would be more convenient,” he says. Before he can leave, the machine prints a receipt. “We apologize for the lack of tea,” it says. At the bottom, in tiny letters, it says, “Free toast with any soup. Price: one hundred yen.” Next to it is a website for people with a food allergy. Kanato checks the ingredients on his smartphone before Yuto can say yes.

ユウトはカフェへ向き「カード払いのほうが便利だな」。自販機は「お茶がなくてすみません」というレシートを出します。下に小さく「スープにトースト無料。百円」と、食物アレルギーの人向けのサイト。かなとはスマホで材料を確認します。

Yuto looks at his cold hands. “All right. Do you have any toast left?”

ユウトは冷たい手を見て「じゃあ、トーストは残ってる？」

A drawer opens. Inside is a little bag with one piece of toast. The three share two cups of soup. Kanato checks that the toast is egg-free on the ingredients page, then breaks it into three pieces. It is warm and very good.

引き出しの小袋に一枚。三人でスープ二杯を分け、かなとはトーストに卵が入っていないと原材料欄で確かめ、三つに割ります。温かく、おいしいです。

The sign asks customers to return their empty cups. Kanato drops his cup into the drawer. A moment later it comes out again. There is a note inside.

「空のカップは返却してください」の札。かなとがカップを引き出しに入れると、メモ付きで戻ってきます。

“Thank you,” it says. “I sell tea, too. I just wanted someone to try my soup.”

「ありがとう。お茶も売ってるんです。スープを試してほしかっただけです」
