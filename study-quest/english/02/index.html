宿題ロボット

The Homework Robot

お手本音声

Kanato and Nozomi are in the kitchen.

A small robot is on the table.

“I am your homework robot,” it says.

“Great! My homework is on the chair,” says Kanato.

The robot looks at the homework. “These questions are hard.”

“Can you help me?” it asks.
