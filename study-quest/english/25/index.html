かけ直すと昨日につながる

Please Call Back Yesterday

お手本音声

Yuto finds an old phone in a box of belongings from when he was a baby. The screen says TUESDAY, but today is Wednesday. He picks up the phone.

“Could you meet me at the library tomorrow, Kanato?” he asks.

Kanato answers, “Tomorrow is Wednesday.”

Nozomi looks at Yuto. “I think you called yesterday's Kanato. Let's do a test.” She calls her own number. “I want you to put an apple in your bag before school tomorrow. The red one next to the radio.”

Nozomi opens her bag. There is an apple inside. Her bag was empty just before she made the call.

Yuto has a dentist's appointment on Friday. “Maybe I can call the dentist yesterday and cancel my appointment,” he says.

The reminder from the dental clinic is still on the screen. Reading the dates is giving Yuto a bad headache. It says when to call back, but not which day to call from.

Before he can call the dentist, the phone rings and a recorded message starts to play.

“Would you please stop moving that appointment? I just got back from the dentist. It took ten minutes.”

“Who sent it?” asks Kanato.

“Me. From Saturday,” says Yuto. He looks at the reminder and changes his mind. “I'm busy, but I can spare ten minutes. I'll go on Friday.”
