未来のユウトから来た展示品

Yuto's Message from the Museum

お手本音声

A message appears on the fridge door. It comes from Yuto, forty years in the future. He is helping a museum put together an exhibit to explain how people used ordinary objects during the last century. He has sent a photograph of one of them.

冷蔵庫のドアに四十年後のユウトから連絡。博物館で前の世紀の日用品の使い方を説明する展示を手伝い、一つの写真を送ってきました。

“It's a metal cupboard that lights up,” reads the museum label. “We don't know why.”

「光る金属の食器棚。なぜ光るかは不明」と札。

“That was my house,” says the fridge. “Can you please tell them what I did there?”

「ぼくの家だった。仕事を伝えてくれる？」

Kanato sends a picture of a carton of milk. Nozomi writes, “The box keeps food cold.” The older Yuto sends a reply. The museum understands, but it would like a record that shows how the family regularly used the fridge.

かなとは牛乳パックの写真を送り、のぞみは「食べ物を冷やす箱」と説明。未来のユウトは、家族がふだんどう使ったかの記録を求めます。

“Is one picture enough to show a typical day?” asks the older Yuto. The older Yuto sends a page from an old family photo album. In one photo, half the street is in their kitchen on a national holiday. The older Yuto has circled the same cupboard in the background of three photos. At home, the fridge opens its door. On the top shelf, a pudding with TUESDAY on the lid is sitting by a bowl of leftover pasta. It finds the date in its records.

「写真一枚でふつうの日が分かる？」と、アルバムの一頁を送ります。祝日に通りの半分の人が集まった写真。背景の同じ戸棚に三枚とも丸。今の家で冷蔵庫がドアを開けると、上の棚に残りのパスタと「火曜日」と書いたプリン。記録から日付を探します。

Kanato checks his wallet for an old shopping list. Before he finds one, the fridge sends its own list. It shows thirty-four pudding purchases, four arguments over the last cup, and one agreement to buy a larger pack next time. In the margin it says, “Especially when your cousins come. They think three cups for the whole family is a terrible idea.”

かなとが財布の買い物メモを探す間に、冷蔵庫が自分の記録を送ります。購入三十四回、最後の一個の言い争い四回、大きいパックにする約束一回。「いとこが来ると特に。家族全員に三個はだめだって」

“Was this a special ceremony?” asks the museum.

「特別な儀式？」と博物館。

“It was Tuesday,” says Kanato.

「火曜日だよ」とかなと。

The next label is better. “This object kept food cold. One household used it to store milk and pudding.” Under that, the older Yuto has added one line of his own: “Of great importance on Tuesdays.”

次の札には「食べ物を冷やした。この家では牛乳とプリンを保管」。未来のユウトは「火曜日には大変重要」と一行追加。

The fridge puts the new label right in the middle of its door.

冷蔵庫はドアの真ん中に貼りました。
