消えたプリン

The Missing Pudding

お手本音声

Yesterday, Kanato put a pudding in the fridge.

This morning, he opened the door. The pudding was gone.

“I didn’t eat it,” said Nozomi.

They looked behind a big bottle and found a spoon.

“I moved your pudding,” said the fridge. “Look in the freezer.”

Kanato found it. “Great. Now I have a pudding rock.”
