消えたプリン

The Missing Pudding

お手本音声

Yesterday, Kanato put a pudding in the fridge. This morning, he opened the door. The pudding was gone.

“I didn't eat it,” said Nozomi.

They looked behind a big bottle and found a spoon. Nozomi smelled it. “I mixed sugar and lemon with that spoon. I baked some cupcakes. They were tasty. The spoon still smells like lemon.”

“I moved your pudding,” said the fridge. “Look in the freezer.”

Kanato found it next to a cheesecake. He tried the spoon. It nearly broke. He reached for a knife. The knife didn't help.

“Great,” he said. “Now I have a pudding rock.”
