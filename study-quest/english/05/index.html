ドアの前のねこ

The Cat at the Door

お手本音声

A cat sits outside the door. “It can jump,” says Kanato, “but it can't open the door.”

“Can you open it, please?” asks Nozomi.

The wind moves the door. There is a loud sound. The cat jumps and hides its face. Nozomi holds the handle still. The cat sits down again.

Kanato opens the door. “Come in! Please sit here.”

The cat does not move. It puts one paw on the door.

“Please close it,” it says. “I like it outside.”
