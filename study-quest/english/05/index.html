ドアの前のねこ

The Cat at the Door

お手本音声

A cat sits outside the door.

“It can jump, but it can’t open the door,” says Kanato.

“Can you open it, please?” asks Nozomi.

Kanato opens the door. “Come in!”

The cat does not move.

It looks at the door. “Please close it. I like it outside.”
