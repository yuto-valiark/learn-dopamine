第11章

鬼の求人広告

The Ogre’s Job Ad

お手本音声

Momotaro went to the island to find an ogre.

Kanato and Nozomi went with him to help.

They found a sign. “We need someone to make lunch.”

“I came here to fight,” said Momotaro.

“I came to eat,” said the ogre. “Can you make noodles?”

They made lunch together. Nobody had time to fight.
