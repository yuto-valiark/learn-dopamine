赤ずきんと住所が多すぎるオオカミ

Little Red and the Wrong Address

お手本音声

Little Red stops Kanato and Nozomi at a corner. “Which path goes to Grandma's house?”

角で赤ずきんがかなととのぞみを呼び止めます。「どの道がおばあちゃんの家に行く？」

A wolf in a yellow vest stops to ask which way they're going. He's trying to explore the town using a map he hasn't quite understood. He holds up the map. Every house on it says GRANDMA.

黄色いベストの狼も行き先を尋ねます。よく分からない地図で町を探検中で、載っている家がみな「おばあちゃん」です。

“My first customer wrote the map,” says the wolf. “She is a grandma, too.”

「最初のお客さんが書いたんだ。その人もおばあちゃん」

Nozomi asks Little Red for the address. “It's 14 Bridge Road,” says Little Red. “Where is that?” “Go straight, cross the bridge, and turn left. It's opposite the bakery.” Kanato walks with the wolf, who is going in the same direction.

のぞみが赤ずきんに住所を聞くと「橋通り十四番」。「それはどこ？」 「まっすぐ、橋を渡って左。パン屋の向かいだよ」 かなとは同じ方向の狼と歩きます。

The wolf folds up a tourist map of the prefecture. “A skateboarder at the port southeast of here drew this line across the bridge,” he says. When he sees a picture of a grandmother in the bakery window, he heads straight for it. The picture advertises the bakery's handmade products, especially its rolls. “Opposite means on the other side of the street,” says Nozomi. He stops. “Good thing you stopped me. The woman in the window looks just like my aunt.”

狼は県の観光地図を折ります。「南東の港でスケートボードに乗った人が、橋に線を描いてくれたんだ」 手作りのパン、特に丸パンを宣伝する窓のおばあちゃんの絵を見て、狼はパン屋へ一直線。「向かいって道路の反対側だよ」とのぞみ。狼は止まります。「止めてくれてよかった。窓の人、おばさんそっくり」

At number fourteen, Grandma takes the bread from Little Red's basket. Then the wolf gives her three more loaves. He has been to three wrong houses this morning and has bought bread at each door to be polite.

十四番でおばあちゃんは赤ずきんのかごのパンを受け取り、狼からもパンを三本。狼は朝、三軒間違えて、気まずくならないよう一軒ずつでパンを買ったのです。

Little Red sells him back two of the loaves for lunch. She gives him his change and lets him count it.

赤ずきんは二本を昼食用に売り戻し、おつりを数えてもらいます。

“That's right,” he says. “Now, which way is my house?”

「合ってる。さて、僕の家はどっち？」
